ABOUT

Traditional ideas, presented with care

Honeybee Saju is a free service that turns the basic vocabulary of Korean Four Pillars into warm, readable reflection.

What we provide

We calculate the pillars from birth details and explain traditional images such as the Five Elements. The result is an invitation to reflect on habits and relationships, not a guarantee about a person's life.

Our boundaries

We do not present Saju as medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice. Important choices belong with evidence, lived context, and qualified professionals.

Your data

Birth details used in the reading are processed in the browser and are not stored on our server. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
