BEGINNER'S GUIDE

A practical introduction to Korean Saju

Saju translates a birth moment into four time pillars. It is a traditional interpretive system, not a scientific test or a promise about what will happen next.

Four pillars, eight characters

The year, month, day, and hour of birth form the four pillars. Each pillar has a Heavenly Stem and an Earthly Branch, which creates eight characters in total. A reading looks at their relationships rather than assigning a life story to one character.

The Five Elements

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water are images for growth, expression, grounding, discernment, and observation. They are not five personality boxes. Their meaning depends on season, placement, and relationship within the full chart.

Use the result wisely

Use a reading to name a question or a habit you want to explore. Do not use it to make medical, legal, or investment decisions. For those decisions, evidence and qualified professional advice come first.
