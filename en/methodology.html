HOW IT WORKS

How a Honeybee Saju result is made

We separate the calculated parts of a chart from the traditional reflection language used to explain it. That distinction helps keep the result useful without overstating what it can do.

What is calculated

Using a solar birth date and an optional birth time, the service calculates the year, month, day, and hour pillars. When the birth time is unknown, the hour pillar is excluded. The calculation runs in the browser; we do not store the entered details on our server.

Element distribution

The characters in the calculated pillars are connected to the traditional Five Elements—Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. The displayed count is a way to see which symbolic images appear relatively often. It does not diagnose health, predict wealth, or define a person.

Interpretation and limits

Reflection prompts combine traditional Saju relationships with practical questions for daily life. They are not a scientific model, medical assessment, legal opinion, or financial recommendation. Important decisions should rely on evidence and qualified professionals.

Feedback

Tell us about a calculation issue or unclear wording at honeybeesaju@gmail.com. Feedback helps improve both the display and the explanations.
